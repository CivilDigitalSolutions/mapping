      How to use Data Mapper

      1. Add data: upload one or more files, or paste rows copied from a spreadsheet. Each file or paste becomes its own dataset.
      2. Check the columns: the app guesses which columns are latitude, longitude, date and time. Change any guess that looks wrong. The preview shows what will be mapped.
      3. Pick a marker: give each dataset its own shape (circle, square, triangle, waypoint pin, flag and others) and colour so you can tell them apart.
      4. Follow the path: when points have dates and times, they're joined in time order with a line. Arrows show the direction of travel. You can turn this off in the dataset's settings.
      5. Replay: press Replay to play the routes back over time. Drag the slider to any moment, use the step buttons to jump from point to point, and choose the speed and how much of the trail stays visible.
      6. Export: press Export to save a PDF report for a client (map, legend, summary and point tables), or a video of the replay for presentations. Videos can keep the map still or follow the route as it moves. The Save video button in the replay bar does the same.
      7. Inspect: select any marker to see its date, time, distance and time since the previous point, and its other columns.

      Coordinate formats understood

      • Decimal degrees: 51.5007, -0.1246
      • Degrees, minutes, seconds: 51°30'02"N 0°07'29"W
      • Degrees and decimal minutes: 51 30.04N, 0 07.48W
      • One combined column, or separate latitude and longitude columns
      • WKT points: POINT(-0.1246 51.5007)
      • Files: CSV, TSV, GeoJSON, GPX and KML

      Dates and times understood

      • 2024-03-05T09:15:00Z, 05/03/2024 09:15, 3/5/2024 9:15 AM, 5 Mar 2024 09:15
      • Separate date and time columns, Unix timestamps and Excel date numbers

      Privacy

      Your data never leaves this device. It is stored in this browser so it's still here next time. The only outside requests are for the map background tiles, and an anonymous visit counter (GoatCounter). The counter uses no cookies and records only that the page was opened and which features were used, such as "exported a PDF". It never sees your coordinates or files.

      Keyboard

      On the map, arrow keys pan, + and - zoom, Tab moves between markers and Enter opens one.
