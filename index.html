      How to use Data Mapper

      1. Add data: upload one or more files, or paste rows copied from a spreadsheet. Each file or paste becomes its own dataset.
      2. Check the columns: the app guesses which columns are latitude, longitude, date and time. Change any guess that looks wrong. The preview shows what will be mapped.
      3. Pick a marker: give each dataset its own shape (circle, square, triangle, waypoint pin, flag and others) and colour so you can tell them apart.
      4. One file, many vehicles: if a file has a column such as Vehicle or Device, choose it under Split into separate datasets by. Each one becomes its own dataset with its own colour.
      5. Colour by value: in a dataset's Style, choose Colour markers and line by speed, time or any number column. Low values are dark purple and high values are yellow. The scale shows on the dataset's card and in exports. Datasets coloured the same way share one scale, so a colour means the same value on each.
      6. Fix bad points: open a dataset's Table to search and sort its points. Tick points and choose Delete selected, or press Edit to correct a point's name, time, position or other values. With a filter on, you can delete every point the filter hides. Each change can be undone.
      7. Filter: open Filter in the Datasets panel to show only a date and time range, certain hours of each day, or points matching a rule such as "Vehicle is VAN-02" or "Speed is at least 30". Everything follows the filter, including replay and exports. Press Clear filter to see everything again.
      8. Stops and problems: a pause sign marks each stop (with how long it lasted), a short "//" break across the line marks a gap in the recording, and a small red triangle marks a possible GPS jump. Select one for details. They're also listed on each dataset's card (List them). Change what counts under Stops and problems in the Datasets panel. To remove a bad GPS reading, open its triangle and choose Remove this point.
      9. Follow the path: when points have dates and times, they're joined in time order with a line. Arrows show the direction of travel. You can turn this off in the dataset's settings.
      10. Replay: press Replay to play the routes back over time. Drag the slider to any moment, use the step buttons to jump from point to point, and choose the speed and how much of the trail stays visible.
      11. Export: press Export to save a PDF report for a client (map, legend, summary and point tables), or a video of the replay for presentations. Videos can keep the map still or follow the route as it moves. The Save video button in the replay bar does the same. Under Your branding, add your organisation's logo and name to put them on every report and video. Empty space around the logo is trimmed for you; use the size sliders to make it bigger or smaller, and the previews show how it will look.
      12. Inspect: select any marker to see its date, time, distance and time since the previous point, and its other columns.

      Coordinate formats understood

      • Decimal degrees: 51.5007, -0.1246
      • Degrees, minutes, seconds: 51°30'02"N 0°07'29"W
      • Degrees and decimal minutes: 51 30.04N, 0 07.48W
      • One combined column, or separate latitude and longitude columns
      • WKT points: POINT(-0.1246 51.5007)
      • Files: CSV, TSV, GeoJSON, GPX and KML

      Dates and times understood

      • 2024-03-05T09:15:00Z, 05/03/2024 09:15, 3/5/2024 9:15 AM, 5 Mar 2024 09:15
      • Separate date and time columns, Unix timestamps and Excel date numbers

      Time zones

      Times that say which time zone they're in (such as 2024-03-05T09:15Z or +01:00) are used exactly as written. For times that don't, choose where they were recorded when you add the file. If they look hours out later, change it in the dataset's Style settings. Use Show times in at the top of the Datasets panel to see every time in one zone, such as UTC.

      Keep your work safe

      Your data is kept in this browser on this device. Use Save project file in the Datasets panel to back it up, or to carry on from another computer with Open project file. If you remove a dataset by mistake, press Undo in the message that appears.

      Privacy

      Your data never leaves this device. It is stored in this browser so it's still here next time. The only outside requests are for the map background tiles, and an anonymous visit counter (GoatCounter). The counter uses no cookies and records only that the page was opened and which features were used, such as "exported a PDF". It never sees your coordinates or files.

      Keyboard

      On the map, arrow keys pan, + and - zoom, Tab moves between markers and Enter opens one.
